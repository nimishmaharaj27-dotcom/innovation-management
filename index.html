How it works

From an early alert to a pickup window

01

Predict surplus early

The AI reads each store's sales, stock, production and expiry data to flag fresh items at risk of going unsold, days before they'd otherwise be written off.

02

Alert staff with a route

Staff get an early alert for each at-risk item, with a recommended route already attached: discount it for sale, or send it to a food-rescue partner.

03

Shoppers buy, food banks collect

Shoppers browse discounted items online and pay; food banks host the pickup window for stock routed their way, on a set collection schedule.

04

Track outcomes

Stores and sustainability teams see reporting on surplus recovered, waste diverted and revenue saved, so recovery routes keep improving.
